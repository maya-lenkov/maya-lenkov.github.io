One internship, several ways to contribute

My work at Digitas spanned building repeatable reporting tools, investigating relationships between datasets, translating audience analysis into recommendations, and researching evidence for a team presentation.

Reporting automation

I identified a repetitive reporting workflow and developed an automation solution using Python and Excel. I also created process documentation, setup and troubleshooting guides, and instructional videos so nontechnical team members could use the workflow independently.

The work included presenting the solution to the team to support adoption. My resume reports a projected 50% reduction in reporting time; this is a projection rather than a measured production result.
